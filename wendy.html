Finding the Perfect Place

The holiday doesn't begin when we cross into Belgium.

It begins on Sunday evening, when everyone's together, the bags are packed and tomorrow's only job is getting to Bruges.

From then on, every day brings another café, another terrace and another opportunity to discover somewhere we'll wish we'd found sooner.

Some people choose where to sit.

You somehow always seem to find the table everyone wishes they'd spotted first.

Whether it's sunshine, a canal view or simply somewhere quiet enough to enjoy the conversation, we're relying on you to find the places that make the week even better.

We've Got a Feeling...

At some point this week, everyone will stop talking for just a few seconds.

It won't be because there's nothing to say.

It'll be because you've found another perfect spot, drinks have arrived, the view is exactly right and nobody quite wants to leave.

We're fairly sure you'll be the first person to smile and quietly say, "This'll do nicely."

We'll know then that you've found another Bruges winner.

Your Mission

  • Find the cosiest café in Bruges.
  • Find the best terrace for a drink.
  • Officially declare when we've found the best view in Bruges.
  • Root out every jewellery shop worth visiting.

Your Week of Perfect Stops

Monday – Find the First Favourite

Duvelorium for the first Belgian drink, ODA rooftop for the views and Mozart for dinner. Three opportunities to discover somewhere we'll want to return to before the week's over.

Tuesday – Wander Beautifully

The walking tour begins at 11:30 am before an afternoon of wandering Bruges. Keep an eye out for hidden cafés, peaceful courtyards and somewhere that deserves a second visit.

Wednesday – Explore Together

The escape room might be indoors, but afterwards there's plenty of time to wander until another perfect terrace appears.

Thursday – The Luxury Day

A relaxed breakfast, time around Dukes' Palace gardens and steak at El Churrasco. This feels like the day most likely to produce the week's favourite table.

Friday – One Last Discovery

Before heading home, there may just be time for one final coffee, one final view and one last place to remember for next time.

Your Objective

Find the one place
with the best view,

My Notes

Somewhere to remember favourite cafés, beautiful views, lovely little discoveries and anything worth returning to next time.
