Finding the Best Stories

The holiday doesn't begin when we cross into Belgium.

It begins on Sunday evening, when everyone's together, the bags are packed and tomorrow's only job is getting to Bruges.

From then on, there will be plenty of plans, but the best parts of the week probably won't be the things anyone carefully organised.

They'll be the unexpected shops, the ridiculous conversations, the places that look brilliant in photos and the moments that become stories before the holiday is even over.

That's where you come in.

We've Got a Feeling...

At some point this week, you'll find something nobody else noticed.

It might be a hidden shop, a perfect photo spot, an enormous waffle or somewhere that looks too interesting to walk past.

You'll persuade everyone to stop, something funny will happen and suddenly it'll become one of the stories everyone remembers.

By the time you get home, you'll already be showing the photos and explaining why Bruges was much more fun than anyone expected.

Your Mission

  • Find the best waffles in Bruges.
  • Discover somewhere worth taking a photo.
  • Find one shop nobody else spots.
  • Make everyone laugh at least once every day.

Your Week of Unexpected Fun

Monday – Start the Stories

The first Belgian drinks, rooftop views at ODA and ribs at Mozart. Plenty of opportunities for the first brilliant photo and the first story worth retelling.

Tuesday – Look Beyond the Obvious

The walking tour begins at 11:30 am, followed by time for shops, hidden streets and unexpected discoveries. Keep an eye out for somewhere nobody else would have found.

Wednesday – Escape and Celebrate

The escape room begins at 2 pm. Whether the group escapes brilliantly or completely falls apart, it should provide enough material for the rest of the week.

Thursday – Make Luxury Fun

A slower day, beautiful surroundings and steak at El Churrasco. The challenge is making sure even the sophisticated moments produce a good story.

Friday – One Last Discovery

Breakfast, shopping and one final wander before heading home. There is still time for one last photo, one last waffle and one more thing nobody expected to find.

Your Objective

Find the moments

the photos you'll still be showing,

that make you want to tell everyone about Bruges when you get home.
