Welcome, Charlotte

Finding the Best Bite

The holiday doesn't begin when we cross into Belgium.

It begins on Sunday evening, when everyone's together, the bags are packed and tomorrow's only job is getting to Bruges.

From then on, every day is another chance to discover somewhere delicious.

Some holidays are remembered because of the places you visit.

The best ones are remembered because of the meals, the cafés you accidentally found and the desserts you still talk about months later.

That responsibility now rests with you.

We've Got a Feeling...

At some point this week, someone will ask, "Where shall we eat?"

You'll make a suggestion.

Everyone will agree.

The food will be outstanding, nobody will regret the choice and someone will quietly say, "We'll have to come back here next time."

That'll be your moment.

Your Mission

  • Find the best bakery in Bruges.
  • Choose a dessert worth sharing.
  • Discover one hidden café.
  • Convince someone to try something new.

Your Week of Great Food

Monday – First Impressions

The first Belgian drinks, rooftop views at ODA and ribs at Mozart. A strong start, but only the beginning.

Tuesday – Café Hunting

The walking tour begins at 11:30 am before an afternoon of cafés, bakeries and hidden streets. Keep your eyes open for somewhere special.

Wednesday – Discover Something New

Escape the room at 2 pm, then reward yourselves by finding somewhere nobody expected to be so good.

Thursday – The Big One

Luxury day, relaxed surroundings and steak at El Churrasco. A serious contender for the best meal of the trip.

Friday – One Last Treat

Before heading home, there should still be time for one final coffee, pastry or sweet treat to finish Bruges properly.
