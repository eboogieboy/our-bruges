A Week at Your Pace

The best holidays aren't measured by how much you manage to fit into them.

They are remembered through the places where everyone relaxed, the meals nobody wanted to end and the unexpected moments that made the whole week feel worthwhile.

The holiday begins on Sunday evening, when everyone's together, the bags are packed and tomorrow's only job is getting to Bruges.

From then on, there is time to wander, time to stop and absolutely no prize for getting anywhere first.

This is a week for noticing when somewhere feels right—and making sure nobody rushes away from it.

We've Got a Feeling...

Somewhere this week, you'll look around, smile and say, “Isn't this lovely?”

When that happens, everyone else will know that we've found one of the moments worth remembering.

We also suspect you'll discover something unexpectedly delicious, encourage everyone to stay a little longer and provide exactly the right amount of sarcasm whenever the occasion requires it.

Your Mission

  • Find one place nobody wants to leave.
  • Officially declare the week's best “Isn't this lovely?” moment.
  • Encourage everyone to stay just a little longer.
  • Discover the best thing you eat all week.

Your Week at the Right Pace

Monday – Arrive Properly

Settle into Bruges with a drink on the Duvelorium balcony, a rooftop stop at ODA and ribs at Mozart. The week begins exactly as it should.

Tuesday – Wander and Notice

The A Taste of Bruges walking tour begins at 11:30 am. Afterwards, there is time for cafés, shops and anywhere that looks too lovely to walk past.

Wednesday – Something Different

A relaxed morning before the escape room at 2 pm. Once everyone has escaped, the evening is free to become whatever feels right.

Thursday – Take Your Time

A slow afternoon in the Dukes' Palace gardens, followed by steak night at El Churrasco. A day designed for good surroundings and an even better meal.

Friday – One Last Lovely Moment

Breakfast, a final wander and enough time to find one last place worth stopping before the journey home.

Your Objective

Find the moment
when everyone is relaxed,
nobody is in a hurry

and you can honestly say, “Isn't this lovely?”

My Notes

A place to remember favourite meals, lovely discoveries and the moments nobody should forget.
