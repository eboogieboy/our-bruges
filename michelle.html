Finding the Best Moments

The best holidays aren't measured by how much you manage to fit into them.

They are found in the places where conversations last longer than expected, meals become evenings and nobody is looking at the time.

The holiday begins on Sunday evening, when everyone's together, the bags are packed and tomorrow's only job is getting to Bruges.

From then on, there is time to wander, time to stop and absolutely no prize for getting anywhere first.

This is a week for noticing when somewhere feels right—and making sure nobody rushes away from it.

We've Got a Feeling...

At some point, it won't be anything that was carefully planned.

It might simply be everyone together, good food on the table, a drink in hand and nowhere else to be.

We're fairly sure you'll be the first person to look around, smile and quietly say, “Isn't this lovely?”

We'll know then that we've found one of the moments worth keeping.

Your Mission

  • Find one place nobody wants to leave.
  • Officially declare the week's best “Isn't this lovely?” moment.
  • Encourage everyone to stay just a little longer.
  • Discover the best thing you eat all week.

Moments Worth Lingering Over

Monday – Settle In Slowly

A drink on the Duvelorium balcony, a rooftop stop at ODA and ribs at Mozart. Plenty of time to arrive properly and enjoy the beginning.

Tuesday – Wander and Notice

The A Taste of Bruges walking tour begins at 11:30 am. Afterwards, there is time for cafés, shops and anywhere that looks too lovely to walk past.

Wednesday – Try Something New

A relaxed morning followed by the escape room at 2 pm. Once everyone has made it out, the rest of the day can unfold at its own pace.

Thursday – Stay a Little Longer

A slow afternoon in the Dukes' Palace gardens before steak night at El Churrasco. Good surroundings, something lovely to eat and no reason to rush.

Friday – One Last Stop

Breakfast, a final wander and enough time to find one last place worth lingering over before the journey home.

Your Objective

Find the moment
when everyone is completely relaxed,
nobody is checking the time

and you can honestly say, “Isn't this lovely?”
