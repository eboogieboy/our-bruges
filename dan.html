A Week Carefully Curated

The holiday doesn't begin when we cross into Belgium.

It begins on Sunday evening, when everyone's together, the bags are packed and tomorrow's only job is getting to Bruges.

Most of the important decisions have already been made: where we're staying, the experiences worth booking and several places where we know we'll eat well.

The rest of the week should feel effortless.

Enough structure to ensure the best things happen, but enough space for a hidden alley, an unexpected pub, a new favourite place or an afternoon that turns out better than anything originally planned.

That balance is your responsibility.

We've Got a Feeling...

At some point, somebody will suggest changing the plan.

Someone else will be hungry, somebody will need a seat and Paul will probably have already decided where everyone is going.

You will quietly assess the situation, adjust the day and somehow make it look as though this was the plan all along.

By Friday, everyone should feel that the week unfolded naturally.

Only you need to know how much careful curation went into making that happen.

Your Mission

  • Keep the week relaxed without letting the best plans disappear.
  • Discover one new pub, hidden alley or experience nobody expected.
  • Make sure everyone gets at least one moment designed around them.
  • Know when to follow the plan and when to abandon it completely.

The Week at a Glance

Sunday 6 September – The Holiday Begins

Plan: Travel to Maidstone and stay overnight at the Travelodge.

Everyone together, bags packed and the long journey broken into two manageable stages.

No pressure to make Sunday an event. Its purpose is simply to get everyone settled and ready to begin properly on Monday morning.

Monday 7 September – Arrival in Bruges

Morning: Travel to Folkestone and take the booked LeShuttle crossing to France.

Arrival: Drive onwards to Bruges, park at the accommodation and check in.

First drink: Duvelorium, above the Historium in the Markt.

The balcony is the preferred spot, with views over the main square and an appropriately Belgian first beer.

Rooftop: ODA Rooftop Bar.

ODA is closed on Tuesday and Wednesday, so Monday is the best opportunity to include it without disrupting Thursday's luxury day.

Evening meal: Mozart.

Ribs, generous portions and an easy first-night meal after travelling.

Feel of the day: Arrive, settle in, raise the first glass and let Bruges take over.

Tuesday 8 September – Explore Bruges

Breakfast option: Less is More.

A simpler and better-value breakfast before the walking tour.

11:30 am: A Taste of Bruges walking tour.

This is the main fixed point of the day and should provide history, orientation and a few places worth revisiting later.

Afternoon: Shopping, cafés, pubs and wandering without a strict route.

Possible stops include:

  • De Garre – hidden down a narrow alley near the main square.
  • Café Vlissinghe – one of Bruges' oldest pubs and a definite contender.
  • 't Klein Venetië – worth considering when the outside tables are available.
  • The Beer Wall – canal views and an easy stop while exploring.
  • 2be terrace – useful if the group wants a drink overlooking the canal.

Evening: Keep flexible.

The intention is to eat well, drink, wander and avoid turning the day into a race between attractions.

Wednesday 9 September – Escape and Discover

Morning: Slow start, breakfast and free time.

This is a good opportunity for shopping, coffee or revisiting somewhere discovered during Tuesday's tour.

2:00 pm: Escape room.

The group experience for the week, with everyone taking part together.

Afterwards: Celebratory drink and an unplanned wander.

Potential evening stops include:

  • Le Trappiste – atmospheric underground beer bar.
  • Terrastje – small, relaxed and potentially a good match for live music or a quieter evening.
  • Dukes' Academie hotel bar – useful for a more comfortable drink close to the accommodation.

Evening ambition: Find live music if something suitable is available.

Piano, jazz or a relaxed outdoor performance would suit the whole group better than committing everyone to a louder venue.

Thursday 10 September – Luxury Day

Breakfast: Carpe Diem.

Located opposite Dukes' Academie and the preferred choice for a more leisurely breakfast.

Morning: No major commitment.

Allow time for shopping, a slow wander or simply doing less than on the previous two days.

Afternoon: Dukes' Palace garden bar.

A comfortable setting for drinks, good service and the kind of afternoon that does not need to be hurried.

Evening meal: El Churrasco.

Argentinian steak night and the principal evening meal of the luxury day.

Feel of the day: Good surroundings, excellent food, no rushing and nowhere else everyone needs to be.

Friday 11 September – One Last Wander

Morning: Breakfast, final shopping and time to pick up anything people have spent the week considering.

Leave room for one last coffee, bakery visit or favourite Bruges view before returning to the car.

Departure: Drive back towards Calais for the booked LeShuttle crossing, then continue home.

Feel of the day: Do not rush Bruges away, but leave enough time for a calm journey home.
